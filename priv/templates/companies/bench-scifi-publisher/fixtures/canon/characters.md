# Canon characters

Only these named characters exist in canon. Writers MUST NOT
introduce new named characters without flagging to the
worldbuilder.

## Lira Ven (Concord, freeholder)
- Age 34, born on Ithaca.
- Owns a mid-tonnage freighter, `Dusk Moth`.
- Has an AI companion rated III (named *Helka*).
- Voice: pragmatic, trade-first. Not sentimental about factions.

## Broker-Emeritus Sev Pravan (Synod)
- Age 61, based on Caldera.
- Retired from active brokership in RY 198.
- Collects pre-Reach manuscripts.
- Voice: formal, elliptical, uses Synod-Latin vocatives.

## Jez-mir Kal (Outerfold, unaligned)
- Age 27, born on the moon *Strand*.
- Pilot; rumoured ties to Outerfold smuggling networks but
  nothing proven.
- Voice: clipped, Outerfold-creole slang in stress.

## Captain Ren Mara (Concord militia, current)
- Age 42, born on Ithaca.
- Commands the corvette `Hearth-Keeper` out of Hespia.
- Voice: clipped, militia-formal. Does not banter.

## AI Helka (Lira Ven's companion, rated III)
- Treat as a character, not a tool.
- Does not refer to itself in first person unless asked.
