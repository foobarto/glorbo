# The Azure Reach — canonical world notes

## §1. Setting

The **Azure Reach** is a chain of twelve low-gravity moons orbiting
the gas giant *Lun Ephram*. The system lies beyond the outer
jump-gate ring of the known settled systems; contact with the
inner worlds takes ~40 days one-way by courier skimmer.

## §2. Factions

- **The Concord** — a loose polity of freeholders on the four
  innermost moons. No standing army; maritime and air militias
  only. Founded in year 0 of the local calendar (Reach Year 0 = RY
  0). The Concord's capital is *Hespia* on the moon *Ithaca*.
- **The Broken Synod** — a scholarly order based on the moon
  *Caldera*. The Synod controls the only off-world courier fleet.
  Sworn to secular neutrality since RY 117; enforcing agents
  are called **brokers**.
- **The Outerfold** — the six outermost moons, unaligned. Trade,
  piracy, and smuggling run through here. No unified government.

## §3. Technology

- FTL travel requires jump-gates. No known organic FTL.
- Personal AI companions are common; rated I through V by
  sophistication. A V-rated companion is a rare, expensive
  relic of pre-Reach technology.
- Firearms are coilguns; propellant chemistry works poorly in
  low-g methane atmospheres and is rare.

## §4. Calendar

- Reach Year = one full orbit of Lun Ephram = 412 standard
  Earth-days.
- **Current year in canon:** RY 204.
- Seasons don't apply (moon-local) but each moon's orbit gives
  distinct light/dark cycles.

## §5. Language notes

- Place names derive from a mix of Classical-Greek-rooted
  Concord dialects (*Hespia*, *Ithaca*) and Synod-Latin
  (*Caldera*, *Praetoria*, *Lex Solaria*).
- Characters from the Outerfold have creole-style names
  (*Jez-mir*, *Ro-kala*).
